How it works

  1. Export one billing month

    Fifteen minutes of your time. We send you the exact clicks for your helpdesk.

  2. Our tool recounts on your machine

    It reads the export where it sits and writes its reports next to it. Only the counts come back to us.

    No conversations, no customer names, no message text ever leaves your laptop.

  3. You get a settlement statement

    Their count, their own published rules, and honest rules — side by side.

    Every disputed conversation is listed by id, with the rule it failed and why, and the dollar variance under each standard. Underbilling is listed too: a statement you can hand to the vendor is one that includes the findings in their favour.
